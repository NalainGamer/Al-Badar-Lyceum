Al Badar Lyceum

Al Badar Lyceum, located at Rasheed Chowk, Muhalla Aman Abad in Jhang Sadar, is dedicated to fostering academic excellence and strong moral values. We provide a modern, supportive learning environment with experienced faculty, interactive classrooms, and diverse co-curricular activities. Our mission is to inspire critical thinking, nurture leadership, and empower every student for a successful future.
